A line blend of iron oxide from 2% to 12% in 0.5% steps, on two clay
bodies, with a slow cool to encourage crystal growth.

The interesting band turned out to be much narrower than expected:
below 6% it is a thin honey, above 9% it goes matte and dry and
slightly unpleasant to touch. Everything worth using lives in about
three tiles.

Running it again with a tighter blend across that band.
