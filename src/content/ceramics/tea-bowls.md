The brief I set myself was: six bowls, one session, no measuring, no
attempt to make them the same. A set held together by the hand that
made them rather than by dimensions.

Five are good. The sixth is too heavy at the foot and I kept it
anyway, because a set where one piece is visibly the worst one is
more honest than a set where I quietly binned the evidence.
