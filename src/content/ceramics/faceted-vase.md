Thrown as a simple cylinder with a slightly thicker wall than I would
normally leave, specifically so there was material to cut away.

Faceting is a commitment. Once the harp goes through the wall there
is no adding it back, and every facet changes the ones next to it —
cut two flats too close and the ridge between them goes knife-thin
and will crack in the bisque.

The tenmoku breaks rust-red over every edge, which is the whole
reason to facet a pot in the first place: you are manufacturing
places for the glaze to do something interesting.
