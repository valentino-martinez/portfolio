Four corners, sixteen tails, and about six hours of paring.

Dovetails are mostly a marking exercise. If the knife line is right
and you cut to the waste side of it every single time, the joint goes
together. The failures are always the moment you stopped believing
the line.

The lid is resawn from one board and book-matched, so the figure
mirrors across the centreline. Getting that match to land on the
actual centre of the lid rather than three millimetres off cost more
thinking than the dovetails did.
