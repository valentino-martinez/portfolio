Thirty-two strips of maple, glued up four at a time because that is
as many as I could get clamped before the glue grabbed.

The top is flat to about half a millimetre over its length, which
took three flattening sessions spread over a year as the slab moved
and settled. The base is drawbored so it can be knocked apart with a
mallet and no glue joint to break.

Deliberately unfinished. A bench that you are worried about marking
is a bench you will not use properly.
