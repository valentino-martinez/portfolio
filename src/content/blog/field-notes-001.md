Starting a running log of small things that do not deserve a post of
their own but that I will regret not writing down.

- Got the block plane properly sharp for the first time. The
  difference between "sharp" and *sharp* is not incremental; it is a
  different tool. Shavings came off in continuous translucent ribbons.
- Glaze test tile 14 came out of the kiln with a break over the rim I
  did not expect. Iron in the clay body, probably. Running it again at
  a slower cool.
- Read half of a book on Swiss typography and spent the rest of the
  evening annoyed at grids. A grid is a set of defaults. The mistake
  is treating defaults as laws.
- The sun through the shop window at about 5pm makes the walnut look
  like it is lit from inside. No photograph I have taken captures it.
  Dithering it did not help. Some things are about the light and not
  the image.
